Crawl Space Water Removal Help in Redondo Beach

Water under the house is easy to miss. Most people find it when the floors feel cold and damp, a musty smell comes up through the vents, or someone opens the access hatch and sees a pond. At that point the batts are typically saturated and the framing overhead has sat damp for several days. We pump the space dry, remove the ruined material and get the wood dried while it is still sound.

Getting the water out is only the first half. Because lumber under a floor is slow to soak and slow to dry, our crew tracks its moisture content with a meter and runs dehumidification for the 3 to 5 days it normally takes to bring the readings down. Finding the cause matters just as much, since a dripping water line, a failed drain or groundwater from a storm will flood the area again unless it is addressed.

What It Looks Like

  • Dark water marks and chalky deposits partway up the block or concrete
  • A first floor with a cool, moist feel and some give when you walk on it
  • Stale, mildew type air coming up around the vents in the floor
  • Sweating pipes and ductwork that drip onto the ground beneath the home
  • Edges of the floorboards lifting into a washboard pattern in the rooms above
  • Fuzzy patches, pale or dark, spreading across the framing or subfloor
Our Process

How We Handle Crawl Space Water Removal

1

Safety Check and Inspection

We check for electrical hazards, sagging ducts and contaminated water before anyone crawls in. Next comes charting the extent of the water and metering the joists and subfloor for moisture.

2

Remove Standing Water

Standing water comes out with submersible pumps and hoses, down to the footing trenches and dips where it gathers. Mud and silt left on the liner are cleared at the same time.

3

Clear Out Ruined Material

We bag up the wet insulation, damaged vapor barrier and loose debris and take it all outside. Our crew then wipes down the uncovered framing and applies antimicrobial to spots with growth or a high chance of it.

4

Final Drying and Checks

Low profile air movers and a dehumidifier run under the house until the wood reaches a normal moisture reading. You get the closing moisture readings plus a plan for keeping water out, after we verify everything by meter.

Hidden Risks

Risks That Are Easy to Miss

Contaminated Water

Do not assume the water below your floor is clean. Groundwater full of soil and waste, or a drain leak, counts as Category 2 or 3, which calls for protective equipment and disinfecting.

Pests Drawn to Moisture

Damp wood and wet soil are exactly what termites, carpenter ants and rodents look for. Drying the space removes much of what attracts them.

Rot You Cannot See

The first soft wood is usually hidden, up where joists meet the subfloor or back at the sill plate. Our crew tests the lumber by hand to locate weak areas.

Air From Below

A good share of the air on your first floor rises from the crawl space, so mold spores and damp odors down there end up in the rooms you live in.

The Damage Timeline

Day one

Puddles Form Underneath

Water collects in the low spots and soaks into the soil. Batts begin drawing moisture upward and the air below the house turns humid quickly.

Hours 24 to 48

Early Mold Growth

Mold can start on damp joists and paper faced insulation. Soaked insulation gains weight and starts to sag off the floor above.

Days 3 through 7

Moisture Moves Upstairs

Humid air from below works its way up into the rooms. The lumber goes on taking in moisture, a stale smell spreads and flooring can begin to cup.

Past one week

Wood Starts Rotting

Decay begins in any framing that never got a chance to dry. Expect softer framing, rusted fasteners, and a wet environment that draws in termites and similar pests.
